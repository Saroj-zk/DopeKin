Frequently Asked Questions
What is a Digital Twin? expand_more
A Digital Twin is an AI-powered version of a creator that can chat, call, and interact with fans 24/7, mimicking the creator's voice and personality.
How do creators earn on DopeKin? expand_more
Creators set subscription tiers or per-message fees for their twins. DopeKin handles all the processing and payouts, letting creators focus on content.
Is the chat private and secure? expand_more
Yes, all chats are end-to-end encrypted and completely private between the user and the digital twin.
Can I create my own Twin? expand_more
Absolutely. Our Creator Studio provides easy-to-use tools to train your twin on your voice and knowledge base in minutes.
